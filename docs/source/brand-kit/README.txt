DaVero AI - Brand Identity Kit
Version 1.0 | November 2026

BRAND POSITION
Enterprise agentic AI focused on the "last mile": the client-specific workflows between platform go-live and delivered business outcomes.

APPROVED MASTER ASSETS
01_Vector_Logos/  Editable SVG lockups, wordmarks, marks, favicons and app icons.
02_Transparent_PNG_Logos/  High-resolution exported PNG versions with transparency.
03_Digital_Assets/  LinkedIn cover, social avatar, OG preview, presentation cover, app favicons.
00_Brand_Guidelines/  Ten-page Brand Guidelines PDF.
04_Design_Tokens/  CSS and JSON palette and type definitions.

PRIMARY BRAND COLORS
Midnight #081630 | DaVero Orange #FF6500 | White #FFFFFF | Cloud #F3F6FA.
Typography: Inter (Bold/ExtraBold display, Regular/Medium body). Obtain fonts through their normal licensed distribution channel; fonts are not bundled.

LOGO USE
On white use DaVero_Primary_Color; on midnight use DaVero_Primary_Reversed.
For product avatars or favicons use DaVero_App_Icon.
Use standalone icon only where the audience already recognizes the DaVero AI name.
Never crop, stretch, rotate or embellish the master mark. Keep 1/4 mark height as clear space around the lockup.

SVG NOTE
SVG wordmarks retain editable text and require an installed Inter typeface for exact typography. For fidelity across environments, use the supplied transparent PNGs or outline the SVG wordmark in your graphics editor before sending it to vendors.

SCOPE
This is an initial production-ready visual asset system. It is not a trademark clearance assessment. Visual examples and lines are proposed brand content; no website, address or business registration is asserted.
