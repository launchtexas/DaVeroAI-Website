How we work

From first conversation to agents in production

Every engagement is led by a Forward Deployed Consultant, a domain expert who works alongside finance and operations leaders from day one.

Step 1 · Weeks 1–5

Embed

A Forward Deployed Consultant embeds with the business, maps the process and identifies which last-mile workflows agents can take over.

Step 2 · Within the 6–10 week embed

Build & harden

We turn each workflow into an agent skill, harden it with rules, controls and edge cases, and business owners validate it before go-live.

Step 3 · Ongoing

Run & improve

We run, monitor and tune the agents every month, and every engagement adds to our workflow library.

Then we expand to the next workflow, entity or function. Advisory and assurance roles remain with existing advisors and auditors.

How we work

From first conversation to agents in production

Every engagement is led by a Forward Deployed Consultant, a domain expert who works alongside procurement and AP leaders from day one.

Step 1 · Weeks 1–5

Embed

A Forward Deployed Consultant maps the procure-to-pay flow, approval matrix and exception queues with procurement and AP leads.

Step 2 · Within the 6–10 week embed

Build & harden

We turn each workflow into an agent skill, harden it with company policies, authority limits and edge cases, and business owners validate it before go-live.

Step 3 · Ongoing

Run & improve

We run, monitor and tune the agents as suppliers, categories and policies change.

Then we expand: new categories, new entities, new supplier segments. Procurement, AP and audit responsibilities remain unchanged.

Controls

Controls your auditors will recognize.

Every agent runs inside the business's controls, with a human sign-off on every result and a full audit trail.

Segregation of duties kept

No agent approves its own output, and vendor bank details never change without human review.

Least-privilege agent access

Each agent gets only the permissions its workflow needs, through scoped, revocable credentials. No shared logins, no standing admin rights.

Guardrails and kill switch

Agents run inside defined rules and thresholds, are monitored continuously, and can be paused instantly by the business.

Client data stays protected

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, never used to train shared models, and stays within approved systems.
